EQUATION STUDIO 1.0 - WINDOWS, OCR OFFLINE

CAI VA CHAY
1. Cai Python 3.11 ban 64-bit (kem Python Launcher). Windows 10/11 x64.
2. Giai nen ZIP. Mo thu muc project.
3. Chay setup_windows.bat. Can Internet de cai thu vien.
4. Chay prepare_models.bat. Can Internet de tai mo hinh mot lan.
5. Chay run_app.bat. OCR trong app chan ket noi mang, khong gui anh toi API.
   Neu chuyen may, can chuan bi mo hinh tren may dich.
   Co the dung menu Cong cu > Tai/chuan bi mo hinh.

SU DUNG
- Win + Shift + S, chup cong thuc; trong app bam Dan screenshot/Ctrl+V.
- Import anh PNG/JPG/BMP/TIFF/WebP; co the chon nhieu anh.
- Chon anh tren dropdown. Tick Quet moi anh de xu ly ca danh sach.
- Anh hai trang canh nhau: tick Hai trang canh nhau. App chia tai giua anh,
  doc het nua trai roi nua phai. Can crop anh neu duong chia khong o giua.
- Trich cong thuc: phat hien cong thuc doc lap va cong thuc trong doan van.
- Chi cong thuc dung rieng: bo qua cong thuc inline.
- Khoanh vung: keo chuot quanh mot cong thuc de nhan dang lai.
- Ctrl + cuon chuot de zoom; keo anh de di chuyen; Vua khung de reset.
- Chon cong thuc: app hien vung anh goc, LaTeX va cong thuc dung lai.
- Sua LaTeX truc tiep. Khong can bam Save rieng cho tung cong thuc.
- Tick cong thuc can xuat. An cong thuc trung chi an dong va loai dong an
  khoi xuat; khong xoa ban goc. Cong thuc trung duoc so sanh LaTeX da bo
  delimiter, khong so sanh tuong duong dai so.
- Copy Equation: dan Ctrl+V trong Word desktop. Clipboard cung cap MathML.
  Tuy phien ban Word, dinh dang clipboard co the khong duoc nhan dang.
  Neu dan thanh text, dung Xuat Word hoac Copy LaTeX:
  Word Alt + = > chon LaTeX > dan ma > Convert > Professional.
- Xuat Word: DOCX chua OMML, co the click vao cong thuc va sua trong Word.
  Chi xuat cac cong thuc da tick va dang hien. Khong xuat anh cong thuc.
- Luu/Mo project .eqstudio: giu ca anh goc va cong thuc, khong phu thuoc
  vao duong dan anh cu. File project co the lon neu import nhieu anh.
- Huy OCR: dung worker, giu ket qua da nhan; cac vung chua xong khong duoc giu.
- Loi mot anh khong chan cac anh tiep theo. Neu chuyen doi Equation loi,
  app thong bao cong thuc nao loi va khong ghi file Word thieu cong thuc.

PHAM VI / GIOI HAN
- Ban dau trich cong thuc, khong xuat lai nguyen trang van ban/bang tieng Viet.
- Khong suy luan cong thuc nao 'lien quan' ve noi dung; nguoi dung tick chon.
- Khong tu sua ky hieu hay kiem chung tinh dung cua cong thuc.
- OCR co the nham mu/pi/vartheta, dau nga, chi so, phan so va ngoac.
  Luon doi chieu anh goc. Diem OCR khong phai xac suat cong thuc dung.
- Mot trang nhieu cot phuc tap: khoanh vung tung cot de giu thu tu mong muon.
- Bang co the sinh cac ung vien cong thuc ngan; bo tick/xoa neu khong can.
- LaTeX-to-Word khong ho tro tat ca macro/package LaTeX. Preview dung MathML;
  Word rendering co the khac ve font/spacing. App bao loi lenh chua ho tro.
- Khong can cai TeX Live/MiKTeX/MathType.
- App tat telemetry cua Hugging Face, Ultralytics va ONNX Runtime truoc khi nap model.
- CPU la mac dinh, khong can GPU. Thu vien OCR va model chiem nhieu dung luong.
- Neu DLL loi tren Windows, cai Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable x64.

BUILD EXE
- Chay build_windows.bat tren Windows trong cung thu muc project.
- Ket qua: dist\EquationStudio\EquationStudio.exe.
- Phan phoi ca thu muc EquationStudio, khong chi file EXE.
- EXE co console de OCR worker gui ket qua; khong dong console khi app chay.
- Model cache khong nhung trong EXE. Tai mot lan tren may dich qua menu Cong cu.
- Windows build/Paste-to-Word can duoc kiem tra tren may Windows thuc te.

DEPENDENCIES / MODELS
- PyQt6 + QtWebEngine (GUI/MathML preview), Pillow, lxml.
- Pix2Text 1.1.7, CPU PyTorch/torchvision, ONNX Runtime, transformers/optimum.
- latex2mathml, mathml2omml, python-docx (Equation Word).
- requirements.txt gom cac thu vien app dung truc tiep; phu thuoc OCR duoc pip
  cai tu metadata cua Pix2Text. installed_versions.txt tao sau setup thanh cong.
- Model cache theo thu vien Pix2Text/CnOCR/CnSTD/Hugging Face, thuong trong
  AppData\Roaming\pix2text, cnocr, cnstd va cache Hugging Face cua user.
- Truoc khi phan phoi thuong mai, xem license cua cac thu vien va tung model.
